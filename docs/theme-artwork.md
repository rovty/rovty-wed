# Wedding theme artwork

Original Rose and Lotus artwork is reused from the existing asset collection. New photographs below are resized and encoded to WebP. Dove, Feather and Moon & Stars have their backgrounds removed; Moon & Stars is also cropped. CSS softly blends these assets into the page. Each adaptation retains its source license.
